00Story

The thing stops being the thing

Pixeldrama is about the moment something stops being what it was. A thing that was one wants to differentiate: it changes its path, and from then on it can never be the same again.

It is not about anyone in particular. It is a general movement. What unites and what separates, the need to be different and the pull to go back. Every path that splits keeps asking where it began.

Followed far enough, that question points to the origin of everything: before there was difference, there was only one thing.

What if we all just become one thing?
